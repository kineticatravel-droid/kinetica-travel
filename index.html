Why Rwanda

Clean, green and easy to love

Compact enough to see a lot in a week, safe enough to relax, and still one of the least crowded places to see gorillas and the Big Five.

01

Safe and welcoming

Ranked first in sub-Saharan Africa for perceived personal safety, with famously warm hospitality.

02

Easy to reach

Visa free for UK passport holders, with direct overnight flights from London to Kigali.

03

Easy to explore

Short distances and good roads, so a game drive or a trek fits between matches.

04

Gorillas and the Big Five

Mountain gorillas in the Virunga volcanoes, savannah in Akagera, lakes and rainforest in between.

05

A modern capital

Kigali is known for its clean streets, good restaurants and a lively coffee culture.

06

A remarkable story

A country defined by renewal, with women holding 63.75% of seats in Parliament, the highest share in the world.

Sport for good

Cricket that gives back

Cricket in Rwanda was started after 1994 by Rwandans returning from neighbouring countries. Today it supports women's and youth empowerment across the country. Every Kinetica cricket tour includes a coaching session with young players, and part of each tour supports the game at grassroots level.

80k+players across the country
20+clubs, with four international grounds
